Website Evaluations

This page evaulates two websites based on CRAP principles of design: Contrast, Repetition, Alignment and Proximity. It includes one website that I think is designed well and one that is terrible.

Apple Music

  • Contrast: The dark/light contrast makes the album art really pop. Plus, main buttons like 'Play' and badges are super clear and easy to find.
  • Repetition: Using the same fonts, icons, card sizes, and menus throughout the app keeps everything looking clean and easy to navigate.
  • Alignment: The grid layout aligns everything into clean rows and columns, so scrolling through albums and playlists feels smooth and easy to skim.
  • Proximity: Song titles, artist names, and album tags are placed right below their cover art, while spacing clearly separates each section.

Craigslist

  • Contrast: There’s barely any contrast on the page. It’s basically just wall-to-wall blue text links on a plain white screen, with no main buttons or pop of color to grab your focus.
  • Repetition: The visual structure is really weak. There are no styled buttons, icons, or cards, just columns packed with endless plain text links.
  • Alignment: The multi-column layout feels really cluttered. Links are crammed into tight vertical lists with weird text lengths, making it hard to skim and exhausting to read.
  • Proximity: Category links like jobs, housing, and services are crammed together with minimal spacing, making the page feel overwhelming and turning the content into a wall of text.
