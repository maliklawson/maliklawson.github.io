Introduction

The founder, Ettore Bugatti, came from a family of artists and sculptors. His rule was simple: "If comparable, it is no longer Bugatti."

Bugatti does not just build fast sports cars, they create exclusive hypercars. Every vehicle they build pushes the boundaries of physics, horsepower and luxury.

Standard cars focus on everyday fuel economy and commuting, while Bugatti focuses on setting world records, breaking speed barriers and treating every sports car like a masterpiece.

Engineering

At the heart of every modern Bugatti is a massive engine designed to produce over 1,500 to 1,800 horsepower

Bugatti made automotive history with their famous W16 engine, which connects two 8-cylinder engine blocks together with four turbochargers—and continues innovating today with high-performance hybrid V16 powertrains.

Aerodynamics

When cars drive faster than 250 miles per hour, two major challenges appear: fighting the wind and keeping the engine cool.

  • Extreme Cooling: A single Bugatti packs up to 10 separate radiators to handle the heat generated by its massive engine.
  • Moving Wings: Active rear wings adjust automatically to press the car down onto the track at speed or tilt straight up as an airbrake during heavy stopping.

Craftsmanship

To handle extreme speeds safely without adding extra weight, Bugatti builds their passenger cabins out of high-grade carbon fiber composite materials.

Carbon fiber is lighter than aluminum but stronger than steel. Every body panel and structural design is hand-crafted and inspected to guarantee the driver is protected inside a bulletproof-strong survival cell.
